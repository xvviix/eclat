An atelier
of intention.

ÉCLAT was founded in Amsterdam's Jordaan district as a quiet rebellion against mass-market floristry. We believe flowers are not mere decoration, but powerful expressions of time, memory and emotion.

Each stem is hand-selected from small growers across the Netherlands and Europe. Every arrangement is designed in the atelier by Margot Laurent and her small team of master florists.
