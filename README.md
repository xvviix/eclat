# ÉCLAT

**Amsterdam's most refined flower atelier.**

ÉCLAT is an intimate, award-winning floristry studio crafting sculptural, seasonal, and emotionally resonant floral arrangements.

---

## Philosophy

We believe flowers are not decoration. They are expressions of time, memory, and presence.

Each bouquet is handcrafted in our atelier in the Jordaan district using only the finest seasonal blooms sourced directly from small, ethical growers across Europe.

## Signature Collections

- **The Eternal** — White peonies, ivory roses, soft sage
- **Blush & Noir** — Dramatic burgundy peonies and blush roses
- **Verdant Reverie** — Lush organic greens and wild florals
- **Golden Hour** — Warm golden tones and luminous blooms
